# Home (/, /fa/)

Scope: the home page and the shared chrome (navbar, dock, footer, primitives, tokens) that blog, projects, lab and 404 inherit. Mode: experience, the person leads and his own git history frames him.
Audience: recruiters and clients first, Iranian employers on /fa/, dev peers via the blog. Job: know who Mahdi is, what he ships, and call or email him.
Voice: first person, nerdy and specific, short sentences, real numbers. Keep: EN + FA (RTL), the live terminal (its own section), Nix and every reaction he has today, day/night, markdown blog, the real screenshots, the keycap toolbox.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A software engineer's first screen is his git history. His story is drawn as a real git tree: `git init: born. hello world!` at the root, a `mechatronics` branch that merges back into main when he graduated, `armani-english` branching off mechatronics, `innolearn` branching off main, lime tips for what is alive now, growing up into his portrait (a large mounted print), Nix small and still in the corner beside it. It grows once, from the root up, the way the earlier edition's branch grew from its bowl, but the subject is rational: a graph an engineer reads every day. Every commit message is a link to that part of Experience; hovering one lights its branch. Refuses the badge + headline + CTA pair + stats hero, the neon hacker desktop, a terminal as the hero, a bone/cream ground, any ikebana, tree-as-plant or Iranian motif, a small round headshot (the owner called it ridiculous), and Nix on top of the tree or changing poses (the owner called the pose-swapping childish).

OWN-WORLD: Sage plaster ground by day (oklch 0.936 0.013 128, pine-grey ink at hue 112), the navy sky by night (oklch 0.215 0.045 262, cool bone ink). Two materials with their own rules. Plastic caps: a 1px lighter rim on top, a 3px darker skirt below, 10px corners, pressable (the skirt collapses and the cap drops 2px); small keys use a 2px skirt and 7px corners. Glass screens: dark green-black by day (oklch 0.21 0.014 128), navy-black at night, 14px corners, a hairline bezel; text on glass is bone, anything alive on glass is lime phosphor in Geist Mono because it is terminal output. Lime otherwise appears only as something lit: the graph's buds and their breathing halos, LEDs (timeline buds, the current nav item, a pressed key), the label of the one primary glass cap. Coral (Nix's tail) marks errors and drafts. No blue or purple in the chrome, no drop shadows (depth is rim and skirt), no colour gradients except the one soft radial field behind the hero graph. Young Serif 400 for headings, Geist for reading, Geist Mono for data, Vazirmatn 400/800 in Persian.

STORY: The visitor meets the sentence about who Mahdi is and, beside it, his story growing upward as a git tree: `git init: born. hello world!` is the root, a Mechatronics branch forks off and merges back at graduation, Armani English branches off it, InnoLearn branches off main, and lime tips mark what he is doing right now. The branches run up into his portrait, Nix beside it. Hovering a commit lights its branch; clicking it scrolls to that experience. Phone, email and the resume are printed beside the tree. Scrolling down: experience as a ruled ledger with lime LEDs, the shipped sites inside small glass screens, the AI loop, the keycap toolbox, skills as keycaps, the live terminal in its own section, the blog, and then the whole width goes to glass: the phone number and the email lit in phosphor at display size, with the call button. The desk edge closes the page.
