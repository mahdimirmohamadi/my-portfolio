# Home (/, /fa/)

Scope: the home page and the shared chrome (rail, footer, primitives) that blog, projects, lab and 404 inherit. Mode: experience, person leads.
Audience: recruiters and clients first, dev peers via the blog. Job: know who Mahdi is, what he ships, and email him.
Voice: first person, nerdy and specific, short sentences, real numbers. Keep: EN + FA (RTL), live terminal, Nix, dark/light toggle, markdown blog.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A tokonoma, not a landing page. One alcove holds three things: Mahdi's photo as the hanging scroll, his career as a single git branch rising from a bronze bowl, and charged empty space that points at the email. Refuses the badge + giant headline + CTA pair + stats hero, and the neon terminal desktop.

OWN-WORLD: Cool sage plaster ground (not cream), pine-grey ink, a dark bronze shelf rail, and one accent, lime, reserved for the three blooms, the email action and the current section. Thin ink rules, near-square corners, a chunky soft serif display over a plain sans; small mono captions set vertically. Night mode is the same alcove at night: charcoal plaster, bone ink, lime unchanged.

STORY: The visitor meets Mahdi's face and one specific sentence, sees the branch of what he has shipped, reads the work as dated sessions, can poke the terminal, and ends at a big plain "say hi".

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left rail (brand, vertical nav, now-note). Alcove: headline and sub left-aligned at 40% width, Email button (lime) and Notes link under it. Right third: photo scroll hanging off-centre, the branch drawn from the bowl on the shelf with three lime buds, Nix asleep on the shelf, a vertical mono caption. Shelf bar closes the viewport.

FORM: ikebana ma arrangement, bold register re-roll 2, lead card. Seed key 6227cd91.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
