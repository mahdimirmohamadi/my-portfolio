# Design System: The Git Graph

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Git Graph"**

A software engineer's first screen is his git log. The site is built on two materials that belong to a working machine, not to a theme: sage plaster for the ground and everything you can press (caps, keys, plates, panels), and dark green-black glass for every screen (the browser windows around the shipped sites, the terminal, code blocks, the contact close). Plastic is molded: a 1px lighter rim along its top edge, a darker skirt under its bottom edge, and it drops 2px when pressed. Glass is a recess with a hairline bezel, bone text, and phosphor for whatever is alive on it. Lime is never paint; it is only ever something lit: the buds on the git graph and their breathing halos, the LEDs on plastic (the Experience buds, the "you are here" dot in the nav, a pressed key), phosphor text on glass, and the label of the one primary glass cap per block.

The first screen carries the whole idea. On one side the person: the sentence, the phone number, the email, the resume. On the other his story as a real git tree: `git init` when he was born (his photo is the root commit), a branch for studying Mechatronics that merges back when he graduated, a branch off it for Armani English, a branch for InnoLearn, three lime tips for what is alive now, growing up into his portrait: him, now. Nix stands small in the corner beside it. It grows once per session from the root up, and every commit message is a link to that part of Experience. Below it the page is compact and scannable: ruled ledgers, small screens, keycaps and index lines printed on the plaster under a chunky soft serif set large. Night is the same room under a navy sky: the shell turns navy, the glass turns navy-black and stays darker than the shell, the ink turns cool bone, and the phosphor does not change.

The world explicitly refuses: drop shadows and blur, colour gradients (the one exception is the soft radial field behind the hero graph), glow as decoration, eyebrows and kickers over headings, card grids, imitation brand marks, monospace as costume, blue or purple in the chrome, the badge + headline + CTA pair + stats hero, the neon hacker desktop, a terminal as the hero, a bone or cream ground, and any ikebana, tree or Iranian motif. This is a product, molded and quiet.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Two materials with their own rules: sage plastic (rim on top, skirt below, pressable, ink text) and dark glass (bezel, bone text, phosphor for what is alive).
- Lime is only ever lit: the graph's buds and halos, bordered LED dots on plastic, phosphor text on glass in Geist Mono, and the label of the one primary glass cap per block.
- Depth is rim, skirt and recess (0-blur box-shadows), never a drop shadow; the only textures are the terminal's raster lines and the radial field behind the graph.
- Young Serif 400 for the human voice on the plaster; Geist for reading; Geist Mono only where the content is literally terminal output or data (the log's refs, the neofetch, captions, tags).
- One authored motion moment (the arrival: the copy rises, the tree grows from the root, one branch at a time); after it, only a quiet commit flowing up each living branch now and then. Everything else is press feedback and short state changes. Nix is still in the hero: one pose, no reactions.
- Full RTL parity: logical properties, the graph and every directional pose mirrored via `--dir`, Vazirmatn 400/800 for Persian.

## Colors

A sage plaster ground with pine-grey ink, dark green-black glass for the screens, three muted branch colours for the graph's lanes, and one saturated yellow-green that reads as light rather than paint; at night the plaster becomes a navy sky and the lime stays exactly where it was.

### Primary
- **Phosphor** (lime): the single accent, and only ever lit. On the graph it is the three buds (a 9px-radius dot with an ink stroke) and their 16px halos breathing at 12–45% opacity. On plastic it is an LED: the Experience buds, the "you are here" dot in the navbar and dock, the bud that walks the AI loop, the LED on a pressed toolbox key. On glass it is text: the neofetch host line, the `$` prompt, the caret, links on a screen, the phone number and the email in the contact close. It is also the label of the one primary glass cap per block, the fill of `.btn--lit` and the hovered resume choice, text selection and the blog reading-progress bar, and at 14% the soft radial field behind the hero graph. Never a surface, never body text on plastic (it fails contrast there).
- **Dim Phosphor** (phosphor-dim): the quieter phosphor for the fact keys in the neofetch, so the values stay the brightest thing on the glass.
- **Stem Lime** (lime-ink): the readable form of the accent on plastic. The refs at the tips of the log (`HEAD -> main`, `innolearn`, `armani-english`), links, focus ring, caret, list markers, hover underlines, the hovered command key's label. At night it becomes Phosphor itself.
- **Seed Ink** (on-lime): text and icons on a lime fill.

### Secondary
- **Glass** (glass, glass-deep, glass-line, glass-bezel): every screen. Glass is the fill, glass-deep the well inside it (browser viewport, URL pill, the primary cap's skirt), glass-line the inset bezel and dividers on a screen, glass-bezel the lighter frame around the neofetch photo and the rules in the contact ledger. Night drops each a step and cools it to hue 262 so the glass stays darker than the shell.
- **Bone / Soft Bone / Faint Bone** (on-glass, on-glass-soft, on-glass-faint): text on glass. Bone for output and headings on a screen, soft for terminal output and leads, faint for captions (`zsh · mahdi@tehran`, `80×24`), the browser bar's dots and Nix's cut line on glass.
- **Main / InnoLearn / Armani lanes** (stem-main, stem-innolearn, stem-armani): the three 3px lines of the git graph, grey-green for main, a muted teal-green for the InnoLearn branch, a muted olive-gold for Armani English. Scoped to the hero stage and the social card; at night they lift a step (night-stem-*) and main cools to hue 250. They are structure, never accent: nothing else may take a lane colour.

### Tertiary
- **Coral** (coral, coral-lit): Nix's tail. Errors and "draft" only: terminal error output (`.t-coral`), the 404 code, draft stickers. Coral-lit is its form on glass and at night. Never decorative.
- **Amber** (amber): the terminal's `help` hint and role words, the lab's caption. On glass and at night it brightens to `oklch(0.8 0.13 72)`. A secondary terminal ink, not a UI accent.

### Neutral
- **Sage Plaster** (shell): the page ground and the navbar bezel; also a cap's hover state and the fill of a hollow commit.
- **Recess** (shell-deep): the plate the toolbox keys sit in, inline-code wells, table heads, avatar wells.
- **Cap** (shell-lift): every cap, key, bubble, panel, the dock at rest, and the root commit's ring fill.
- **Rim** (shell-rim): the 1px highlight along the top edge of plastic and the 2px lip of the desk edge.
- **Skirt** (shell-skirt): the darker 3px skirt under a cap, the 1px border of every cap and screen, the Experience stem and the AI loop's stem, the desk edge, the hairline underline under phone and email.
- **Hairline** (shell-line, aliased as rule): 1px dividers on plastic, plate borders, inline-code borders, the navbar's closing line.
- **Pine Ink / Soft Ink / Faint Ink** (ink, ink-soft, ink-faint): headings, the log messages and primary text / leads, secondary log rows, nav and captions on the graph / dates, refs' neighbours, metadata, legends. Faint ink clears 4.5:1 on the shell.
- **Ink Rule** (rule-strong): the stroke of every hollow commit and of the root's ring, the ring of an LED at night, dashed "again" labels, the amber and lime sticker outlines, Nix's cut line at night.

Legacy names (`--plaster*`, `--bg*`, `--surface*`, `--text*`, `--edge*`, `--bronze*`, `--oxblood`, `--cream`, `--accent`, `--link`, `--focus`) are aliases onto these tokens so blog, project, lab and 404 pages inherit the world; write new surfaces against the shell / glass / lime names.

### Named Rules
**The Lit, Not Painted Rule.** Lime appears only where something is lit: a bud on the graph, a bordered LED dot on plastic (Experience bud, nav "here" dot, pressed-key LED), phosphor text on glass (set in Geist Mono because it is terminal output), the label of the one primary glass cap per block, or the fill of the one lit cap on a screen. If a second thing on screen wants lime, it gets ink.

**The Two Materials Rule.** Every surface is either plastic (shell family, ink text) or glass (glass family, bone text). Text on glass is bone; anything alive on glass is phosphor. Text on plastic is ink; anything alive on plastic is lime-ink or an LED. Never bone on plastic, never ink on glass.

**The Brand-on-Hover Exception.** Tool marks sit in ink until touched: hovering a skill key lights its logo and border and tints the key 12% in the tool's own brand colour (simple-icons hex; near-black brands fall back to ink at night). The AI toolbox and AI loop marks show their real colours at rest because they are the subject there. These third-party colours are the only blues and purples on the site, and they never become UI chrome.

**The One Field Rule.** Every fill is a flat token. The only gradients are the terminal's raster lines (a repeating 1px line at 14% black every 4px: a texture, not a colour) and the soft radial field behind the hero graph (lime at 14% by day, `oklch(0.4 0.09 262 / 0.5)` at night, fading out at 72%). Nothing else may use one. No blue or purple in the UI.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Young Serif (with Georgia, serif), weight 400 only
**Body Font:** Geist (with system-ui), weights 400, 500 and 600
**Label/Mono Font:** Geist Mono (with ui-monospace), weights 400 and 600
**Persian:** Vazirmatn 400 / 800 replaces both display and body under `dir="rtl"` (Geist Mono has no Persian glyphs; Persian text inside a screen, the log's meta and Persian tags switch to Vazirmatn too)

**Character:** a chunky, soft, slightly old-fashioned serif is the human voice printed on the plaster, set larger than a portfolio usually dares; a plain modern sans does the reading, including the log messages beside the graph; the monospace is literally what the machine prints: refs, dates, captions, the terminal. The contrast is warmth over precision, never ornament.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (400, step-5, 1.08): the contact close title only ("Say hi.").
- **Hero** (400, clamp 2.5-4.4rem, 1, max 12ch): the one hero sentence. Persian: clamp 2.1-3.6rem, 800, max 16ch.
- **Headline** (400, step-3, 1.08): section titles ("Work", "Projects", "Skills", "Or ask the terminal") and the company names in the Experience ledger.
- **Title** (400, step-2): project titles, the AI toolbox title, "Before that", the resume picker question.
- **Subtitle** (400, step-1): index-line project titles, "Elsewhere", the contact lead, prose h3.
- **Body** (400, step-0, 1.6, 48-68ch): leads and prose. Leads in ink-soft (or soft bone on glass).
- **Body strong** (600, 0.98rem): button labels, arrow links, ledger labels, the reach values.
- **Log message** (600, 0.95rem, 1.3): the commit messages beside the graph at the tips and the root; the rows in between drop to 500 in ink-soft. 0.82rem / 1.2 under 480px.
- **Log meta** (400, 0.72rem, tabular, Geist Mono): the ref or `branch · date` under each message, in faint ink, lime-ink at the tips; Vazirmatn 0.76rem in RTL; 0.66rem (0.72rem RTL) and one line with an ellipsis under 480px.
- **Small** (400, step--1 to 0.95rem): metadata, nav links, footer, key legends' partners.
- **Caption mono** (400, 0.75rem, tabular): data only: screen captions, tags, key legends, PDF meta, the `EN · FA` on the resume ticket.
- **Terminal** (400, 0.86rem, 1.65): everything printed on the screen; the input is never under 16px on phones.
- **Lit display** (600, clamp 1.25-2.6rem, 1.2, -0.02em, tabular): the phone number and the email in the contact close, phosphor on glass with a faint bloom.

### Named Rules
**The Honest Mono Rule.** Monospace appears only where the content is literally terminal output or data: the log's refs and dates, the screen, the neofetch, the command keys, tags, captions, key legends, and the two lit contact lines (which are terminal output at display size). Headings, nav, buttons, prose and the log's commit messages are never mono.

**The One Weight Display Rule.** Young Serif is set at 400 with -0.015em tracking and balanced wrapping. Emphasis comes from size and position, not bold. In RTL, headings switch to Vazirmatn 800, line-height 1.35, tracking 0, and body line-height rises to 1.9.

## Layout

A sticky 60px navbar is printed on the bezel (shell fill, closed by a 1px shell-line hairline); anchors land just under it (`scroll-padding` 72px; each section cancels its own top padding via `scroll-margin`, plus `--anchor-extra` where a Nix stands above the heading). Content sits in a centred column (max 1200px, gutters 32px, 18px under 768px). At 900px+ the navbar carries the section links; below that they move to a floating five-item dock at the bottom (a cap in thumb reach; the footer reserves 80px for it) and the navbar keeps the brand, the call key and the switches. The phone number shows inside the call key from 1180px.

The hero is two columns at 1000px+ (copy `minmax(0, 1fr)`, the stage `minmax(440px, 540px)`), the row `min(100svh - 74px, 880px)` tall, both columns vertically centred, 5rem apart; the stage sits at the inline-end. The stage is two rows: Nix's spot (200px tall) over the graph. The graph is a two-column grid: a 150px SVG with three lanes at x=34 (main), 74 (innolearn) and 114 (armani-english), and beside it the HTML log, one 64px row per commit, eight rows from y=40, newest first (536px in all). A full-bleed 14px desk edge closes the viewport, and the same desk edge closes the page above the footer. Under 1000px the columns stack (copy first, then the stage centred at full width, Nix 170px); under 480px Nix is 150px, the graph takes 14px of inline-start padding so he can stand inside the gutter, and the labels shrink to one line.

Sections are separated by 5rem (space-2xl). Inside them: the Experience ledger (a 3px skirt-coloured stem at the inline-start with 24px lime buds; story and "what I built there" cap side by side at 960px+), projects with screenshots paired 1fr 1fr at 900px+ (an odd count gives the lead print the full row, and it sits beside its note from a 760px container), the AI loop as five 72px nodes on a stem that lies down at 900px+ (with a dashed retry loop under it) and the toolbox plate as 2 then 4 keys at 820px+, skills as label/key rows (8rem label column from 560px, two columns at 960px+; the list is LTR in both languages), the terminal in its own section (note 0.8fr / screen 1.2fr at 960px+, with Nix typing under the note), the blog teaser, the close as a full-bleed glass band (7rem label column from 700px, Elsewhere with a 9rem title column and two-column list from 760px), and "More nerdy stuff" as a chat list in a panel (0.9fr / 1.1fr at 900px+). Spacing follows the 0.25 / 0.5 / 0.75 / 1 / 1.5 / 2.25 / 3.5 / 5 / 7rem scale.

**The Ledger Rule.** Lists of work, skills, links and posts are rows divided by 1px hairlines (shell-line on plastic, glass-line on glass), not boxes. A row earns its height with content, not padding.

**The Log First Rule.** The hero is the person beside his graph: on desktops the words sit at the inline-start and the graph at the inline-end; on phones the words come first and the graph keeps its lane pitch underneath. The hero never becomes a badge + headline + CTA pair + stats stack, and never a terminal.

## Elevation & Depth

The system has no drop shadows. `--shadow` and every legacy shadow token resolve to `none`, and nothing uses blur. Depth is molded into the two materials with 0-blur box-shadows only: plastic gets a 1px lighter rim inset along its top edge and a solid skirt below it (3px for caps, 2px for small keys, collapsing when pressed); glass gets a 1px inset bezel line and the same rim along its top. A plate is a recess (a 2px inset skirt along its top). The only glow is the phosphor bloom, a soft text-shadow on lit display text on glass (the contact phone and email, the neofetch host line), and it is legibility for a lit character, not elevation. The graph's halos are lime discs animated by opacity, not blurred. Nix's cut line (four 1px 0-blur drop-shadows) is a sticker edge, not a shadow.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Cap** (`box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 var(--shell-rim), 0 3px 0 var(--shell-skirt)`): every cap, ghost button, the "what I built there" panel, the AI loop's nodes, the dock, the chat list, the resume picker and its choices.
- **Cap, pressed** (`translate: 0 2px; box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 var(--shell-rim), 0 1px 0 var(--shell-skirt)`): the skirt collapses as the cap goes down (90ms).
- **Small key** (`box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 var(--shell-rim), 0 2px 0 var(--shell-skirt)`): command keys, nav switches, skill keys, stack tags, `kbd`; pressed: `translate: 0 2px` and the skirt goes to 0.
- **Linked tag** (`box-shadow: 0 2px 0 var(--shell-skirt)`): a tiny key; pressed: 1px down, 1px skirt.
- **Plate** (`box-shadow: inset 0 2px 0 var(--shell-skirt)`): the recess a row of keys sits in.
- **Screen** (`box-shadow: inset 0 0 0 1px var(--glass-line), inset 0 1px 0 var(--shell-rim)`): the terminal, browser windows, `.win` panels; the contact band uses `inset 0 1px 0` and `inset 0 -1px 0` glass-line for its top and bottom bezels.
- **Glass cap** (`box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 var(--glass-bezel), 0 3px 0 var(--glass-deep)`): the primary button on plastic; at night the rim is glass-line and the skirt `oklch(0.08 0.01 120)`.
- **Lit cap** (`box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 oklch(0.97 0.12 124), 0 3px 0 oklch(0.55 0.15 128)`): `.btn--lit` and the hovered resume choice.
- **Phosphor bloom** (`text-shadow: 0 0 14px oklch(0.905 0.2 124 / 0.32)`; the neofetch host line `0 0 10px … / 0.35`): lit display text on glass only.
- **Graph field** (`radial-gradient(closest-side, oklch(0.905 0.2 124 / 0.14), transparent 72%)`; night `oklch(0.4 0.09 262 / 0.5)`): the one soft field, behind the hero graph, like light from a screen.

### Named Rules
**The Rim and Skirt Rule.** Things are molded, they do not float. Depth is a 1px rim on top and a solid skirt below, and pressing a thing collapses its skirt. No drop shadows, no blur, no glow on plastic.

**The Bloom Only on Glass Rule.** The phosphor bloom exists only on lit display text on a screen. Never on plastic, never on a button label, never on body text. A bud on plastic glows by opacity (its halo), never by blur.

## Shapes

Molded, not square. Caps and buttons take 10px corners; small keys (command keys, nav switches, skill keys, stack tags) 7px; toolbox keycaps 12px; screens, plates and the largest plastic panels (the resume picker, the chat list, the toolbox plate) 14px; tags and printed date labels 5px; small controls, log rows and inline code 5-6px; the URL pill in a browser bar is a full pill. Every cap and screen is closed by a 1px shell-skirt border (glass-deep for the glass cap). Circles are the LED and commit family: 7px nav and dock dots, 8px LEDs, 9px key LEDs, 8px hollow "built" dots, the tree's 12px hollow commits (2.5px ink-rule stroke), the 20px merge ring, 16px tips (2px ink stroke) inside 30px halos, the 24px root ring around a 12px dot,  18px hollow school node and the walking bud, 24px Experience buds, the 72px AI nodes, the browser bar's three hollow 10px dots, round avatars. Rules are 1px hairlines for structure, 3px for the tree's branches, the Experience stem and the AI loop's stem, 2px dashed for the retry loop and the "again" label. Lines on the graph have round caps and joins; a fork is a cubic curve that leaves main 50-60px before the join. The neofetch photo sits in an 8px frame with a glass-bezel line. Arrows are drawn 16px strokes (1.6 width, round caps) and mirror in RTL; the graph mirrors with `scaleX(var(--dir))`.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** keep lime to what is lit: the graph's buds and halos, bordered LED dots on plastic, phosphor text on glass (Geist Mono), the label of the one primary glass cap per block, the one lit cap on a screen, and transient state (selection, reading progress, a hovered resume choice); use lime-ink for any lime text or link on plastic.
- **Do** put the phone number wherever the email is: navbar, hero, the close, the footer, the terminal's `contact`.
- **Do** build every plastic thing as a cap: shell-lift fill, 1px shell-skirt border, a 1px rim inset on top and a solid skirt below (3px caps, 2px small keys), and make it press (2px down, skirt collapses, 90ms).
- **Do** build every screen as glass: glass fill, 1px shell-skirt border, an inset glass-line bezel, 14px corners, bone text, phosphor for what is alive; put code, terminals, screenshots and the contact close on glass.
- **Do** draw the graph honestly: lanes at 34 / 74 / 114 with a 64px row pitch, hollow commits for the past, lime buds only for what is alive now, the three lane colours for the lines and nothing else, and only dates `profile.ts` sources.
- **Do** separate content with 1px hairlines (shell-line on plastic, glass-line on glass) and let the plaster breathe between sections (5rem); close the hero and the page with the desk edge.
- **Do** use Young Serif 400 for headings and Geist for everything readable; switch both to Vazirmatn (800 for headings) in RTL, and Persian text on glass and in the log's meta too.
- **Do** use logical properties and multiply every directional translate, rotate or scale by `var(--dir)`; mirror the graph, arrows and directional Nix poses in RTL.
- **Do** give motion a reduced-motion and `html[data-lite]` path that shows the final state, and keep it to transform, opacity and stroke-dashoffset.
- **Do** show tools by their real marks: simple-icons in currentColor (brand colour on hover), official site icons for Antigravity and Hermes Agent, or the name alone when no mark exists.
- **Do** write like the person talks: first person, conversational, a little funny. Persian copy is colloquial (محاوره), not formal.

### Don't:
- **Don't** add drop shadows, blur, glow on plastic or colour gradients; depth is rim, skirt and recess, the terminal's raster lines are the only texture, and the radial field behind the hero graph is the only soft field.
- **Don't** paint a surface lime, set lime body text on plastic, or bloom text anywhere but lit display text on glass.
- **Don't** put an eyebrow or kicker line above a heading; a caption is for data and places only.
- **Don't** build card grids; use ruled ledgers, small screens, keycaps and index lines.
- **Don't** draw imitation logos or placeholder marks.
- **Don't** set headings, nav, buttons or the log's commit messages in monospace to look technical; mono is for what the machine prints.
- **Don't** put bone text on plastic or ink text on glass; each material keeps its own ink.
- **Don't** introduce blue or purple outside the navy night shell and third-party brand marks.
- **Don't** invent commits, buds or dates on the graph, and don't reuse the lane colours anywhere else.
- **Don't** give a section more than one authored animation (the arrival in the hero, the loop in the AI section, the keys typing once); everything else is feedback-sized.
