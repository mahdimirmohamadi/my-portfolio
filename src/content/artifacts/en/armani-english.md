The main storefront for Armani's educational products and services: many pages, a wide content structure, and a catalogue that keeps growing.

## What it took

- Keeping a large Next.js codebase on the **latest framework and library versions**, without breaking the store.
- Technical and structural SEO work that **improved search visibility significantly**.
- Core Web Vitals fixes that cut page load times by **30%**. That's the Sluggish Page Div, defeated in Chapter 2.
- Typed forms end to end with React Hook Form and Zod, and client state kept small with Zustand and React Query.
