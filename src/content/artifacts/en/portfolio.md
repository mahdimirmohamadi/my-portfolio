You're reading it. It's a manga volume where the Persian edition is the original printing, a narrator named Simo, and a photo inked by a small script.

- **Astro** renders every page to static HTML. The home page ships almost no JavaScript.
- Illustrations are hand-written SVG, screentone is CSS, and the paper grain is baked at build time.
- The **Chaikhaneh** (tea house) is reserved for a lofi radio and a pomodoro timer. They'll live in a React island that keeps playing while you turn pages.
