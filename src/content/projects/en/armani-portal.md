A student portal made of several complex sub-systems, each one practically its own app:

- **Real-time exams** with **AI-powered scoring**.
- A **smart study planner** that helps students keep a rhythm.
- **Study clubs** where learners meet around a course.
- **Live webinars**.

Built with React and TypeScript, with Chakra and Shadcn for UI, SWR and Jotai for data and state, and Framer Motion for the moments that deserve motion.
