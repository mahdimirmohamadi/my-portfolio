InnoMeet is the live side of InnoLearn: an online meeting platform built for classes, not for corporate calls. It shares a Turborepo monorepo with the LMS.

## My part

I built a big chunk of the classroom itself:

- **Chat** plus live **video, audio and image streaming**.
- **Live quizzes and polls** a teacher can drop into the middle of a class.

Real-time UI is its own sport: every state can change under you, from any direction, at any moment.
